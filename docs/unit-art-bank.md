## Banked Designs for Future Units

1. **`office-clerk-v1-bust-pro.png`**
   - **Visual:** Silver-ash hair, thin rimless glasses, tailored white & charcoal blouse with emerald collar pin.
   - **Chris's note:** Great shirt design and cool vibe — too high-class for Tier 2 Office Clerk; save for a **higher-tier Support / Special / R&D unit**.
   - **Alpha status:** Enclosed neck/hair white pocket cleaned.

2. **`office-clerk-v3-threequarter-pro.png`**
   - **Visual:** Sleek dark high ponytail, structured charcoal vest with emerald armband, holding a translucent holographic ledger slate (3/4 framing).
   - **Chris's note:** Science/data vibe feels like a **higher-tier unit** (e.g. Data/Research/Support specialist).

3. **`call-center-veteran-v1-bust-pro.png`**
   - **Visual:** Long dark-blue hair, sleek single-ear holographic comms headset with glowing emerald mic tip, tailored corporate blazer.
   - **Chris's note:** Classy and well-rendered; bank for a **higher-tier comms / dispatch / PR / support unit**.

4. **`call-center-veteran-v3-threequarter-pro.png`**
   - **Visual:** Male specialist with silver-streaked dark hair, high-collar acoustic operator jacket, boom headset, and wrist console (3/4 framing).
   - **Chris's note:** Strong design and thought — save for an **Intel / Scouting unit** (or style-match into the Intel roster).

5. **`p2-banked-v4-outfit-rework-dark.png`** *(plus original outfit reference `call-center-veteran-v4-full-satire.png`)*
   - **Visual:** High-fashion tailored charcoal-and-emerald corporate comms jacket with structured shoulder pads, wrap-around collar trim, and floating holographic ticker, reworked onto a **short platinum-blonde pixie-cut character** (1/2–3/4 framing) so there is zero resemblance to any Secretary.
   - **Chris's note:** Loved the clothing design from Vet V4; use this reworked non-Secretary character/outfit for a **higher-tier Special or Support unit**.
